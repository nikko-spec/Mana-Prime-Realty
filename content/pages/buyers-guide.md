## 1. Reservation
A reservation fee holds the unit or lot while paperwork is prepared. This is
typically credited toward your down payment.

## 2. Down payment
Paid over a fixed term (often 12-24 months) directly to the developer or
seller, depending on the property.

## 3. Financing
Bank financing, Pag-IBIG, or in-house financing — we'll walk you through
which options fit the property and your timeline.

## 4. Turnover and titling
Once fully paid, the property is turned over and the title transfer process
begins. We stay involved until the title is in your name.
